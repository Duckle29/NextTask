**An unofficial Nextcloud Tasks client for Android, with nested subtasks, offline sync and undo.**

NextTask brings your [Nextcloud Tasks](https://apps.nextcloud.com/apps/tasks) to your phone, with subtasks
nested as deep as you need and full use without a connection.

## Features

- Subtasks within subtasks, with fold and unfold
- Long-press and drag to reorder tasks, or to move them into and out of subtasks
- All your task lists in a side drawer, one swipe away; create and delete lists, even offline
- Check off, add and delete tasks and subtasks, with undo and redo
- Due dates, with overdue tasks in red
- Optional reminder notifications for your tasks' reminders, which Nextcloud itself doesn't send
- Works offline: changes are kept on your device and sync automatically when you are back online
- Completed tasks are collected in their own list
- Export your tasks as a standard `.ics` file

**Secure sign-in.** You sign in through your browser. NextTask never sees your password: it receives a
separate app password, which is stored encrypted on your device and removed from your server when you sign out.

**Privacy.** No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your tasks go only to the Nextcloud server you choose.
Read the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).

**Requirements.** Android 8.0 or newer, and a Nextcloud server with the Tasks app.
