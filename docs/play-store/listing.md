## App name (8/30)

NextTask

## Short description (70/80)

Unofficial Nextcloud Tasks client: nested subtasks, offline sync, undo

## Full description (1010/4000)

NextTask brings your Nextcloud Tasks to your phone, with subtasks nested as deep as you need and full use without a connection.

FEATURES
• Subtasks within subtasks, with fold and unfold
• All your task lists in a side drawer, one swipe away
• Check off, add and delete tasks and subtasks, with undo and redo
• Works offline: changes are kept on your device and sync automatically when you are back online
• Completed tasks are collected in their own list
• Pull down to refresh
• Export your tasks as a standard .ics file

SECURE SIGN-IN
Sign in through your browser. NextTask never sees your password: it receives a separate app password, which is stored encrypted on your device and removed from your server when you sign out.

PRIVACY
No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your tasks go only to the Nextcloud server you choose.

REQUIREMENTS
A Nextcloud server with the Tasks app.

NextTask is an independent project and is not affiliated with Nextcloud GmbH.
Note: NextTask is vibe coded using claude sonnet 5.5 and opus 5.5
Source code: https://github.com/Duckle29/NextTask
